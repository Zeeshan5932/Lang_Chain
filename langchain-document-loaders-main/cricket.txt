Cricket

Beneath the sun or floodlight's gleam,
Cricket lives like a waking dream.
A field of green, a willowed sound,
Where legends rise and tales are found.

The bowler runs with rhythmic pace,
The batter stands with steady grace.
A leather ball, a wooden crack,
The crowd erupts, no looking back.

A drive through cover, swift and clean,
The finest shot you've ever seen.
A spinner turns, the stumps take flight,
The stadium roars in pure delight.

A hundred scored, the helmet raised,
The fans all cheer in joyful praise.
Win or lose, the spirit stays,
Cricket shines through all our days.
